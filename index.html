ROBOT LEARNING & EMBODIED INTELLIGENCE

About me

I study how robots understand the world and turn that understanding into action.

I am a Ph.D. student at Tongji University and Shanghai Innovation Institute. My research centers on three connected directions: geometry-aware vision-language-action models, humanoid WAM, and humanoid locomotion and control. Across these directions, I use reinforcement learning and real-world evaluation to study reliable robot behavior.
