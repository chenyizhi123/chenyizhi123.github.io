About

I am a Ph.D. student working on robot learning, with a focus on geometry-aware VLA policy learning, humanoid perceptive locomotion, reinforcement learning for embodied control, and real-world robot system evaluation.

My current work studies how robot policies can use geometry, terrain structure, and proprioceptive state to make more reliable decisions in manipulation and locomotion.
